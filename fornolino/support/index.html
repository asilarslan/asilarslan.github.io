Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I make a pizza?

Read the order ticket, then follow the stations: pick the dough size and swipe to stretch it, paint the sauce with your finger, grate the cheese, tap or drag toppings onto the pizza (some orders want different halves), bake it in the oven and take it out when the bell rings, then swipe across the pizza to slice it and serve.

Why did I get a low score?

Each pizza is scored on dough shape, sauce and cheese coverage, the right toppings in the right places, bake level, and clean, even slices. The results screen shows what to improve. Turn on slice guides in Settings for help with cutting.

Which mode should I play?

Story grows your pizzeria day by day. Relaxed has no customer timers. Challenge is for high scores.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only, with an automatic backup copy. Deleting the app removes your progress.

Accessibility

Settings include reduced motion, slice guides, vibration, separate music and sound volumes, and English or Turkish language. You can replay the intro from Settings at any time.
