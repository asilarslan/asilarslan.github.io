Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Kitbench is a single-player game. We do not run servers and do not collect your personal information ourselves. The free Android version shows ads through Google AdMob, which is explained below.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked kits, workshop upgrades, your collection, partial and Free Build saves) and your settings (volume, graphics, accessibility options) only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device by the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Android)

The Android version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show an occasional full-screen ad after a delivery and an optional ad you can choose to watch to double your coins. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, app interactions and diagnostics. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your Choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form; you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings. The game itself has no accounts, analytics or tracking of its own, and the iOS and desktop versions contain no ads.

Permissions

On Android the game requests vibration (haptic feedback) and, for ads, internet access and the advertising ID. It does not access your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or files.

Purchases

Kitbench contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; ads are restricted to general-audience content.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change; it will be updated before any such release. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.
