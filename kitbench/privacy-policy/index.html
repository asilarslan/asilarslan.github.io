Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Kitbench is an offline game. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked kits, workshop upgrades, your collection, partial and Free Build saves) and your settings (volume, graphics, accessibility options) only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device by the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No Network, Accounts, Ads or Analytics

The game does not make network requests, does not require an account, and contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking. We do not use an advertising identifier and do not track you across apps or websites.

Permissions

On Android the game only requests permission to vibrate, for haptic feedback when parts snap together. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Purchases

Kitbench contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change; it will be updated before any such release. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.
