Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I build a kit?

Tap the kit box to open it — the parts spill into your tray. Parts that glow are needed for the current step. Drag one close to the model; a green shadow shows where it will click into place. Tap the lightbulb any time for a hint.

How do I move the camera?

Drag empty space to orbit, pinch to zoom, and use two fingers to pan or twist. The on-screen buttons do the same. While holding a part, tap with a second finger to rotate it.

A part won't snap

Check that it belongs to the current step (it glows), rotate it so its connector faces the right way, and bring it closer. You can undo, or detach a placed part, at any time.

I ran out of coins

Deliver finished toys for orders to earn coins. If you put a kit away before finishing it, its cost is refunded. There are no timers and nothing can be lost.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Progress, unfinished builds and Free Build sketches are saved automatically. Deleting the app removes your progress.

Can I replay the tutorial or intro?

Yes — both can be replayed from Settings, and the intro also from the main menu.
