Slide Studio AI

Support

Write to support@asilarslan.com with your device, the app version (Settings › About) and what happened. We reply in English or Turkish.

Restoring a subscription

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Cancelling

iPhone, iPad, Mac: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Slide Studio AI. Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Where are my presentations?

On your device, inside the app. Export them to PowerPoint, PDF or images to share or keep a copy elsewhere.

Deleting data

See the privacy policy. To delete everything on our server for your app right away, email us.
