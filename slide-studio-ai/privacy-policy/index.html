Slide Studio AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Slide Studio AI ("the app") creates, edits, presents and exports presentations on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

Your presentations stay on your device

Presentations, imported files, photos and recordings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. We have no copy of them. They may be included in your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) if you have backups turned on. Deleting a presentation, or the app, removes it from the device.

Work done on your device

  • Reading documents (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, text), recognising text in photos and scanned pages, and the basic deck builder all run on your device.
  • Voice dictation uses your device's speech recogniser. When on-device recognition is available it is used; otherwise the system speech service (Apple's or Google's) may process the audio, and the app tells you before you start. The microphone is used only while you are recording.
  • The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to scan a page or pick photos.

AI writing (Premium, only when you choose it)

When a Premium subscriber asks the app to write or rewrite slides with AI, and has agreed to it in the app, the following is sent over an encrypted connection to our server at asilarslan.com and from there to OpenAI, which generates the slides:

  • the topic and the text you provided (for example pasted notes or text read from your document), and
  • your choices for the deck: language, number of slides, audience, tone and style.

Photos, audio, files themselves and your other presentations are not sent. Our server keeps the request and the result for at most 24 hours — so a retried request is not charged twice — and then deletes them. OpenAI processes the text under its API data usage policy: data sent through the API is not used to train its models and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. You can turn AI writing off at any time in Settings; the app then builds decks on your device only.

Anonymous app identifier

To use AI writing the app creates a random installation identifier. It is not linked to your name, email address, Apple ID or Google account. Our server stores only a hash of it, the platform (iOS, macOS or Android), when it was created and last used, and for each AI request the type of request, the model used, its size in tokens and its cost — never its content after the 24 hours above. This is used to apply daily limits and prevent abuse.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. So that Premium AI writing works, the app sends the store's signed purchase record to our server, which checks it with Apple or Google and stores the product, the transaction identifier, the expiry date and whether it was a test purchase.

No advertising, no tracking

The app shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share your information. Like any website, our server's hosting provider may briefly keep standard access logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security.

Deleting your data

Presentations are deleted in the app. Data on our server about AI requests expires on its own as described above. To have everything linked to your app identifier deleted right away, email us; the deletion removes the request history, usage records and subscription record for that identifier.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@asilarslan.com

Gizlilik Politikası

Slide Studio AI (iPhone, iPad, Mac ve Android) sunumlarını yalnızca cihazında saklar; bizde kopyası yoktur. Belge okuma, fotoğraf ve taranan sayfalarda metin tanıma ve temel sunum oluşturma cihazında çalışır. Sesle yazma cihazdaki tanıyıcıyı kullanır; cihaz üzerinde tanıma yoksa sistem konuşma hizmeti (Apple veya Google) sesi işleyebilir ve uygulama bunu başlamadan önce söyler.

AI ile yazma (Premium, yalnızca sen seçersen): Konu, verdiğin metin ve sunum tercihlerin (dil, slayt sayısı, hedef kitle, üslup, stil) şifreli bağlantıyla asilarslan.com sunucumuza ve oradan slaytları yazan OpenAI'ye gönderilir. Fotoğraf, ses, dosyaların kendisi ve diğer sunumların gönderilmez. Sunucumuz isteği ve sonucu en fazla 24 saat tutar, sonra siler. OpenAI API ile gönderilen veriyi modellerini eğitmek için kullanmaz; kötüye kullanım denetimi için 30 güne kadar saklayabilir. AI ile yazmayı Ayarlar'dan istediğin zaman kapatabilirsin.

Uygulama adına, e-postana ya da Apple/Google hesabına bağlı olmayan rastgele bir kurulum kimliği oluşturur; sunucu yalnızca bu kimliğin özetini, platformu ve her AI isteğinin türünü, modelini, boyutunu ve maliyetini (içeriği değil) günlük sınırlar ve kötüye kullanımı önlemek için saklar. Ödemeleri Apple veya Google yapar; ödeme bilgilerini görmeyiz. Premium'un çalışması için mağazanın imzalı satın alma kaydı sunucumuzda doğrulanır ve ürün, işlem kimliği ve bitiş tarihi saklanır.

Reklam, reklam/analitik SDK'sı ve takip yoktur; bilgilerini satmayız veya paylaşmayız. Sunucudaki tüm verilerinin hemen silinmesini istersen bize yaz. Uygulama 13 yaş altı çocuklara yönelik değildir. İletişim: support@asilarslan.com
