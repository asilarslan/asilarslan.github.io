Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I take a device apart?

Aim at a part: the prompt tells you what holds it ("Held by 4 fasteners", "Remove the cooler first", "Requires Screwdriver"). Hold Interact (E / A / the round button) on a thumbscrew to undo it by hand. Other screws need the Screwdriver. Once nothing holds a part, pull it off.

How do I use the hammer?

Switch to the Hammer (2, Q or the mouse wheel; Y or D-pad on a gamepad; the tool button on touch). Hold Use (left mouse / RT / Swing button) to wind up (the ring around the crosshair fills), then release to swing. A longer wind-up hits harder.

Why did a part lose value?

Every part has a condition, shown in the prompt. Hammer blows and hard drops lower it. Glass shatters completely; plastic and circuit boards break off. For the best salvage, unscrew carefully.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, mouse to look, E to grab or interact, right mouse to throw, hold R to rotate a held object, 1–3 to pick a tool. Gamepads are supported. On iPhone and iPad, the left side of the screen is a move stick and the right side looks around.

Settings and accessibility

The pause menu has look sensitivity, field of view, camera shake (set it to 0 to turn shaking off), head bob, invert look, effects volume and language (English / Türkçe).

Where are my settings saved?

On your device only. Deleting the game removes them.
