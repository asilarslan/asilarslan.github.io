Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Gadget Wreck Lab is an offline game. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves only your settings (look sensitivity, field of view, camera shake, head bob, effects volume, language, graphics quality) and, in future versions, your workshop progress, on your device in the app's private storage. This data is never sent anywhere by the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple, Google or computer settings. Deleting the game deletes this data.

No Network, Accounts, Ads or Analytics

The game does not make network requests, does not require an account, requests no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos), and contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not use an advertising identifier and do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Gadget Wreck Lab contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by the store (Apple, Google or Steam); we do not receive your payment details.

Platform Services

If a version adds optional platform features such as Game Center, Google Play Games, Steam achievements or cloud saves, those features are provided by the platform under its own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that release.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.
