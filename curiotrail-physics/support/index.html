How does a lesson work?

Each experiment follows six steps: Observe, Predict, Experiment, Explain, Challenge and Complete. Pick a guess before you test it; there is no wrong guess. Then change one thing (the ramp angle, the push force or the surface) and run it again. The Explain step shows what your measurements mean.

How does my garden grow?

Finishing an experiment grows a plant on the Journey map. Trying different surfaces, objects and masses helps it bloom fully.

Can I watch the intro again?

Yes: Settings → Watch the intro again.

Accessibility

Settings include text size (100%, 115%, 130%), reduced motion, sound captions, haptics, language (English / Türkçe) and separate music and effects volume.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app removes it. Settings → Reset all progress starts over.
