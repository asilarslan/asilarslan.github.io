Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

CurioTrail: Physics is an offline educational game. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information, from anyone, including children.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves your learning progress (completed experiments, challenge results, garden growth) and your settings (language, text size, reduced motion, captions, haptics and volume) on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No Network, Accounts, Ads or Analytics

The app makes no network requests, needs no account and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not use an advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites. On Android it declares only the vibration permission, used for optional haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings.

Children

CurioTrail is designed for learners of all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information, no personal information is collected from children. There are no chats, links to social media, ads or purchases inside the app.

Purchases

The app contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, loot boxes or gambling mechanics.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Parents may contact us at any time.

Changes and Contact

If a future version adds a feature that changes this policy, this page will be updated before that version is released. Questions: asilarslan93@gmail.com.
