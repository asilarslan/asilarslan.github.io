Support

Help with scanning, solutions, subscriptions, and privacy.

Get a Better Solution

  • Photograph one complete problem at a time.
  • Keep signs, exponents, roots, fractions, units, and diagrams in focus.
  • Avoid glare, shadows, cropped lines, and steep camera angles.
  • If a symbol was misread, ask the tutor to use the correct transcription.

Camera or Gallery

Camera access can be changed in Android Settings. Gallery selection uses Android's system picker. If an image cannot be opened, choose it again or take a new photo.

Subscriptions

Purchases are managed by Google Play. Use Restore Purchases in the app if Premium does not appear after reinstalling. Ensure the same Google account used for purchase is active in Play Store. Cancel or manage renewal from Google Play Subscriptions.

AI Accuracy

AI can make mistakes. Check important work and ask a teacher when an explanation conflicts with course material. The app does not guarantee grades or exam results.
