Bellhaven: Christmas Old Town – Support

Help and contact for the game on Android, iPhone, iPad and Steam. Privacy Policy

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? Write to meltembkr@gmail.com and include your device model.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the game or clearing its data resets your progress.

Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. Choose Türkçe on the title screen or in Settings.

Are there ads?
The phone and tablet versions show a full-screen ad now and then at a natural break, and an optional video can double the daily gift. The Steam version has no ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy options.
