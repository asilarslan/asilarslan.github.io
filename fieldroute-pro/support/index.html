FieldRoute Pro – Support

FieldRoute Pro measures agricultural fields and plans how a machine should work them, for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Measuring a field

  • New field: tap the plus button, then tap the corners of the field on the map.
  • Adjust: drag a corner to move it; drag the small dot in the middle of an edge to add a corner. Tap a corner and use the minus button to remove it. Undo and redo are in the toolbar.
  • Exclusion zones: use the zone button to mark areas that are not worked, such as ponds or pylons.
  • GPS: the record button adds corners while you walk or drive the edge. Positions less accurate than about 15 m are not recorded.

Planning a route

  • Open Plan, choose the field and your machine, and tap Generate routes.
  • Enter your real working width and turning radius under Machine; the templates are only starting values.
  • If a route is marked Not drivable, the turns do not fit in the headland. Add headland passes (the app suggests how many) or check the turning radius.
  • Press play to watch the operation; drag the timeline to move through it.

Good to know

Route figures (time, fuel, cost, coverage) are estimates calculated from the route and the machine values you enter. Areas are agricultural estimates, not a legally certified survey. The app is a planning aid and does not steer or control any machine.

Subscription

FieldRoute Pro adds unlimited fields, comparison of route alternatives and file export. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store account settings or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions. Use Restore purchases on the offer screen after reinstalling.
