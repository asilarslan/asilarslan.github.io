What the app is

FieldRoute Pro is a planning and measuring aid for agricultural work. It does not steer, guide or control any machine.

Estimates, not guarantees

  • Areas and distances are agricultural estimates based on the points you enter or record. Phone positioning is typically accurate to a few metres. Results are not a legally certified cadastral survey.
  • Routes, times, fuel, costs and coverage figures are calculated from a model using the values you enter. Real results depend on the machine, the field, the crop, the weather and the operator. Route ordering is heuristic and is not guaranteed to be the best possible.
  • Satellite imagery can be out of date.

You are responsible for checking that a planned route is safe and suitable before working a field, and for operating machinery safely and lawfully.
