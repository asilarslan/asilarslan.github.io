FieldRoute Pro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

FieldRoute Pro ("the app") is published by Asil Arslan. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

The app has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your fields, machines, routes and settings are stored on your device and are not sent to us. We do not collect any personal data.

Information stored on your device

  • Field boundaries, names, notes and other details you enter
  • Machine profiles and planning settings
  • App preferences such as units and appearance

This information stays in the app's storage on your device. On Android it may be included in your own Google device backup if you have backups enabled. Deleting the app deletes this information from the device.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location while the app is in use, to show where you are on the map and to record a field boundary when you start a recording. Location is processed on the device only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not use location in the background.

Map imagery

Satellite imagery is downloaded from Esri (ArcGIS). To show the map, the app requests image tiles for the area you are viewing, so Esri's servers receive the requested area and your IP address, as with any online map. See Esri's privacy information. Choosing the "Offline geometry" map style makes no such requests.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. The app checks with the store on your device whether a subscription is active.

Files you export

Exported files (GeoJSON, KML, GPX, CSV) are created only when you choose to share a field, and go only where you send them.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
