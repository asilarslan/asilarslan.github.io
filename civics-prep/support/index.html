Support

Questions or found a mistake? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model and OS version.

Which test does the app cover?

The 2025 civics test with 128 questions. If you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, you take the 2008 test with 100 questions; check the date on your notice and the USCIS study page.

How does the practice interview work?

The officer asks up to 20 questions. You pass with 12 correct answers; the interview stops after 9 wrong answers. If you are 65 or older with 20 years of permanent residence, turn on 65/20 special consideration under More: 10 questions from the starred list, 6 correct to pass.

Why do some answers mention my state?

Some answers depend on where you live. Pick your state under More and add your senators, representative and governor. Elected officials change; check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.

I found a wrong answer

Please email us the question number. We follow the official USCIS list and fix errors in the next update.

Restore my Pro purchase

Open Pro and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only, and in your device backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Soporte

¿Preguntas o encontró un error? Escriba a support@asilarslan.com con el modelo de su dispositivo y la versión del sistema.

¿Qué examen cubre la app?

El examen de educación cívica 2025 con 128 preguntas. Si presentó el formulario N-400 antes del 20 de octubre de 2025, le corresponde el examen de 2008 con 100 preguntas.

¿Cómo funciona la entrevista de práctica?

Hasta 20 preguntas; aprueba con 12 correctas y termina con 9 incorrectas. Con la opción 65/20: 10 preguntas de la lista marcada, 6 correctas para aprobar.

Restaurar mi compra Pro

Abra Pro y toque Restaurar compra con la misma cuenta de Apple o Google.

¿Dónde se guarda mi progreso?

Solo en su dispositivo y en su copia de seguridad. Si borra la app, se borra su progreso.
