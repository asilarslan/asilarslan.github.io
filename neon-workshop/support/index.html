Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I play?

Pick the Pen and tap each corner of the chalk pattern; points snap to it. Tap the last point again (or double-tap) to finish a tube. Then switch to Wiring and drag cables from the transformer through every tube and back to the transformer's return post. Flip Power to light the sign.

How do I make curves and arcs?

With the Pen, press and drag at a point to pull a smooth curve. With the Arc tool, drag from one end to the other, then drag again to bend it and lift your finger to place it.

The sign won't light. What's wrong?

When you flip Power, a pulse follows the current and stops where the problem is: a loose cable end, a cracked tube, or a transformer carrying too much glass (each transformer shows its load at the bottom left). Fix it there and try again.

Zoom, pan and undo

Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to pan (mouse wheel and middle-drag on a computer). Use the ↶ and ↷ buttons to undo and redo.

Accessibility

Settings (⚙) include reduced motion (no flicker or camera movement), colour labels on every glass colour, language (English / Türkçe), and volume.

Where are my settings saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app removes them.
