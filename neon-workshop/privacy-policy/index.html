Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Neon Workshop is an offline game. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves only your settings (volume, language, reduced motion, colour labels, display options) on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device by the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No Network, Accounts, Ads or Analytics

The game does not make network requests, does not require an account, requests no device permissions, and contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not use an advertising identifier and do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Neon Workshop contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

Platform Services

If a future version adds optional platform features such as Game Center, Google Play Games, or cloud saves, those features are provided by the platform under its own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that release.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.
