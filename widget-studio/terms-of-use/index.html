Using the app

Widget Studio lets you design widgets for your iPhone that can also appear in CarPlay and StandBy where Apple supports them. You're responsible for the photos and text you add, and for having the right to use them.

Pro subscriptions

Weekly and yearly Pro plans are auto-renewing subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Lifetime purchase

The Lifetime option is a one-time purchase that unlocks Pro features for as long as the app is available, on devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

What Pro includes

Pro unlocks premium designs, photo filters, custom colors and typefaces, and unlimited saved designs. Features may be added or changed over time.

CarPlay

How, where, and whether widgets appear in CarPlay is controlled by Apple's software and your vehicle. Widget Studio can't guarantee a particular layout or availability. Always keep your attention on the road and set up widgets while parked.
