Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email support@asilarslan.com.

When contacting support: include your iPhone model, iOS version, your car model if it's about CarPlay, and the app version (Settings › Version).

Add the widget to CarPlay

  1. Save a design in the app.
  2. Connect your iPhone to your car with CarPlay at least once. Do this while parked.
  3. On your iPhone, open Settings › General › CarPlay and choose your car.
  4. Tap Widgets and turn on Show Widgets.
  5. Tap Add Widget and add the Studio Widget from Widget Studio.

Common questions

The widget isn't in the list

Open Widget Studio once after installing or updating so iOS registers the widget, then reopen the list. If it isn't listed as optimized, look under Other.

Which design does the widget show?

Your default design, set in My Widgets › Make Default. Where iOS lets you edit a widget, you can pick a different saved design for it.

Does it work in every car?

It needs iOS 26 and a CarPlay system that supports the Widgets screen. CarPlay decides widget size and placement.

Why don't clocks show seconds?

iOS doesn't let widgets refresh continuously. Clocks are scheduled minute by minute in advance.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Is my data collected?

No. There is no account, and photos stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
