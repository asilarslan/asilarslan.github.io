# Reply Privacy Choices

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

You control what content you submit to Reply. This page explains the controls available in the app and how to make a privacy request to Asil Arslan, the provider of Reply.

## Delete data linked to your installation

Open **Reply → Settings → Delete My Data**. This erases local reply history, resets AI-processing consent, and requests deletion of the installation record, retry cache, audit metadata, and rate-limit associations held by the Reply proxy.

Deleting the app removes its local data from that device, subject to device backups you control, but does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage subscriptions in **iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions**.

## Control what you submit

- Use Apple's system photo picker to select only the image you want to provide.
- Review and edit recognized or pasted text before requesting a reply.
- Remove names, contact details, or sensitive information that is not needed.
- Deny or change camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.
- Reply does not read messaging apps in the background and never sends a message for you.

## Make a privacy request

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent for future AI processing.

Email [support@asilarslan.com](mailto:support@asilarslan.com?subject=Reply%20Privacy%20Request) with the subject **Reply Privacy Request** and tell us:

1. Which right you want to exercise.
2. The country or region where you live.
3. Only the information reasonably needed to locate the relevant installation, support request, or transaction.

Do not email private conversation content, a full screenshot, payment-card information, an Apple Account password, or a device passcode. We may ask for limited additional information to verify a request and protect users from unauthorized disclosure or deletion.

We will acknowledge and respond within the period required by applicable law. If we deny a request, we will explain why and describe any available appeal or complaint route. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

## Sales, targeted advertising, and tracking

Reply does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. Because those practices do not occur, there is no separate sale/share opt-out switch.

## AI processing consent

Before the first AI request, Reply explains that reviewed conversation text, context, and settings will be sent through our proxy to OpenAI to generate a result. You can decline and continue using non-AI areas of the app. After giving permission, use **Settings → Delete My Data** to withdraw it for future requests; the app will ask again before a later AI request.

Withdrawal does not affect processing already completed lawfully. See the [Privacy Policy](/reply/privacy-policy/) for processing, retention, international transfer, and subprocessor details.
