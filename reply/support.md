# Reply Support

Reply helps you turn a selected screenshot, photo, or pasted conversation into reply suggestions you can review, refine, and copy.

## Quick start

1. Open Reply and choose **Add Screenshot**, **Take Photo**, or **Paste Text**.
2. Review the imported content and remove anything you do not want to submit.
3. Add context and choose a tone.
4. Generate reply ideas, select one, and refine it if needed.
5. Review the final wording, then copy it. Reply never sends a message for you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can't Reply see all of my chats?

Reply does not connect to or read messaging apps. You decide what to provide by selecting a screenshot/photo or pasting text.

### Why is camera or photo access requested?

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. The photo picker lets you choose the specific image you want to use. You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Pasted text does not require photo access.

### Is my content sent to an AI provider?

Yes, when you request a reply, recognized or pasted conversation text, context, and reply settings are sent through our service to OpenAI as described in our Privacy Policy. Text recognition happens on your device and the screenshot/photo itself is not uploaded. The app asks for permission before the first AI request. Retention, subprocessors, and deletion choices are explained at <https://asilarslan.github.io/reply/privacy-policy/>.

### Does Reply send messages automatically?

No. It creates suggestions. You must review, copy, and send any message yourself.

### A reply does not feel right. What should I do?

Try a different tone, add concise relationship/context information, or refine the suggestion. Do not use a reply that is inaccurate, unsafe, or inappropriate. AI output can be wrong.

### Generation failed or is taking too long

Check your connection, keep the app open, and try again with a clear image or shorter pasted conversation. If the issue continues, include your app version, iOS version, approximate time, and the displayed error code in a support email. Do not attach a private conversation unless necessary and authorized.

### How do I restore Premium?

Open **Settings → Restore Purchases** while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. A restore cannot move a purchase between different Apple Accounts.

### How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the iOS **Settings** app, tap your name, tap **Subscriptions**, choose Reply, and follow Apple's cancellation controls. Deleting Reply does not cancel the subscription.

### How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. Visit <https://reportaproblem.apple.com/> and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

### How do I make a privacy request?

Use **Settings → Delete My Data**, visit <https://asilarslan.github.io/reply/privacy-choices/>, or email support@asilarslan.com. Tell us the request you want to make and provide only the information needed to locate the relevant installation or transaction. Read the full policy at <https://asilarslan.github.io/reply/privacy-policy/>.

### Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Indonesian.
