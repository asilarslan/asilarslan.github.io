# Reply Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026

Reply is provided by Asil Arslan (“Reply,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), based in Türkiye. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Reply iOS app, our support channels, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

## 1. What Reply does

Reply creates suggested replies from content you deliberately provide, such as a chat screenshot, another photo, pasted text, optional context, a goal, a language, or a tone. Text recognition for selected images runs on your device. The image itself is not uploaded. The app does not send messages on your behalf and does not read other apps' conversations in the background.

## 2. Information we process

### Content you choose to submit

Selected screenshot/photo bytes are processed temporarily on your device to recognize text and are not sent to our servers or AI provider. We process the recognized or pasted conversation, context, instructions, tone, language, refinements, and other text you submit. This text may include information about you or another person. Please submit only content you are authorized to use and avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

### Generated content

We process the reply suggestions and refinements created in response to your request so we can return them to the app and provide any history feature included in the version you use.

### Device and service information

We may process an installation-scoped identifier, app version, device/operating-system version, language, request time, network response information, quota state, and limited security diagnostics. Our servers necessarily receive an IP address when your device connects. Whether any of this information is retained, and for how long, is described in Section 6.

### Subscription information

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank details. The reviewed app version reads product, transaction, entitlement, expiration, revocation, and offer-eligibility information from StoreKit on your device to unlock Premium and restore access; it does not send purchase history to our service. Apple separately handles App Store purchase data under its policies.

### Support information

If you contact us, we process your email address, the content of your request, and any diagnostic information or attachment you voluntarily include. Do not send private chat screenshots unless they are necessary for support and you are authorized to share them.

## 3. How information reaches us

The app handles image bytes locally when you select or capture an image. We receive information when you submit recognized or pasted text, request or refine a reply, change a setting that synchronizes with the Service, or contact support. Purchase and restore results are handled on device through StoreKit in the reviewed version. We do not continuously monitor your photo library, camera, clipboard, contacts, or messaging apps.

The system photo picker can let you provide selected items without granting broad photo-library access. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. Clipboard access is initiated by your paste action.

## 4. Why we process information

We use information to:

- provide, personalize, and return the reply generation you request;
- use text extracted on device from a selected image and follow your tone, context, and language choices;
- operate subscription entitlements, quotas, and Restore Purchases;
- secure the Service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain availability;
- respond to support and privacy requests; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you to provide requested features, your consent where requested (including the first disclosure to an AI processor), our legitimate interests in security and service reliability, and compliance with law. You may withdraw consent for future processing, but this does not affect processing already completed lawfully.

## 5. AI processing and sharing

When you ask Reply to generate or refine a reply, the recognized or pasted conversation text, context, and instructions travel through our secured proxy service and are shared with OpenAI, L.L.C. to produce the result. Image bytes are not included. Before the first AI request, the app explains this sharing and asks for your permission.

OpenAI's privacy information is available at <https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/>. Our integration uses the Responses API with `store: false`, so response application state is not retained for later retrieval. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train or improve its models unless the API customer explicitly opts in. Under OpenAI's standard API controls, limited prompts, responses, and related metadata may nevertheless be retained in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, unless a different approved data-control arrangement applies. Processing may occur in the United States and other locations where OpenAI or its subprocessors operate.

We may also disclose limited information to infrastructure, hosting, security, and support vendors acting for us; to professional advisers under confidentiality; or when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or complete a corporate transaction. Privacy choices and deletion instructions are available at <https://asilarslan.github.io/reply/privacy-choices/>.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, use conversation content for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, subject to legal, fraud-prevention, dispute, and security requirements.

| Information | Production retention |
| --- | --- |
| Submitted recognized/pasted text, context, and prompts | Not stored by the Reply proxy after the request completes; OpenAI's standard abuse-monitoring retention described above may apply for up to 30 days |
| Selected screenshot/photo bytes | Not uploaded; temporarily processed on device for text recognition |
| Generated replies and refinements | Encrypted in the app's local history until you delete them; temporarily cached by the proxy for safe request retry for up to 24 hours |
| Security and operational logs | Reply audit metadata without conversation/reply text for up to 30 days; hashed rate-limit records for up to 2 days; inactive installation records for up to 365 days |
| StoreKit purchase history | Not sent by the reviewed app to our service; Apple applies its own retention policies |
| Support correspondence | Up to 24 months after the request is resolved, unless law or an active dispute requires longer |
| Residual encrypted backups after deletion | If production backups are enabled, they are rotated out within 30 days and are not restored except for disaster recovery |

Content stored only on your device remains until you remove it in the app or delete the app, subject to device backups you control. Use **Settings → Delete My Data** to erase local history and request immediate deletion of the installation record, retry cache, audit metadata, and rate-limit associations held by our proxy. Removing the app alone may not erase information lawfully retained by processors. To request access or deletion, visit <https://asilarslan.github.io/reply/privacy-choices/> or email support@asilarslan.com. We may ask for limited information to verify and locate the relevant installation or transaction.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to submit any screenshot, photo, or text; deny camera/photo access in iOS Settings; edit a suggestion before copying it; manage your subscription in your Apple Account; and stop future use by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may also appeal a denied request or complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. Some information may be retained where law permits or requires it.

Residents of jurisdictions with specific privacy laws may use the same contact methods. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, we do not offer a sale/share opt-out. If our practices change, we will update this policy and the available controls before the change applies.

## 8. International transfers

The Service and its processors may operate outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we rely on contractual protections such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent lawful transfer mechanisms. Reply has not appointed an EU or UK representative because it does not currently meet the conditions requiring one; contact support@asilarslan.com if you believe this assessment should be revisited for your use.

## 9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information, including encrypted network transport and access controls appropriate to the Service. No system is completely secure. Keep private or highly sensitive information out of submissions when it is not needed, and contact us if you believe your information has been compromised.

## 10. Children

Reply is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support@asilarslan.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Do not submit a minor's private conversation or image without lawful authority.

## 11. AI output and sensitive decisions

Generated replies can be inaccurate, inappropriate, or incomplete. Reply is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, safety, or emergency advice. Review every output before using it and do not rely on it for a high-impact or emergency decision.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Service, law, or providers. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required. Material changes apply prospectively unless law permits otherwise.
