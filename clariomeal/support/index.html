Common Questions

How do I analyze a meal?

Open the Scan tab, choose a meal photo, and tap Analyze. ClarioMeal will estimate calories, macros, ingredients, confidence, hidden-calorie flags, and useful correction prompts.

Why does ClarioMeal show a calorie range?

Meal photos often hide portion depth, oils, sauces, cooking methods, and ingredients. A range is more honest than a single exact number when the image has uncertainty.

What are hidden-calorie flags?

Hidden-calorie flags call attention to items that photos often miss, such as dressing, oil, sauces, fried preparation, sugar, or bowl depth.

Can I trust every AI estimate?

No. AI results are estimates and can be wrong. Always review the result, adjust portions, and use your own judgment. ClarioMeal is not medical advice.

Why are products not loading on the paywall?

Make sure you are connected to the internet and signed into the App Store with a valid Apple ID. If plans still do not appear, restart the app and contact support.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings and tap View Pro Plans, then use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select ClarioMeal.
