ClarioMeal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

ClarioMeal helps users estimate calories, macros, confidence ranges, ingredient portions, and hidden-calorie risks from meal photos. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may process, how it is used, and what choices users have.

Information We Process

  • Meal photos you choose from your photo library or capture for analysis.
  • Meal analysis results, including estimated calories, calorie ranges, macros, ingredient names, portions, confidence scores, hidden-calorie flags, correction prompts, and coach notes.
  • Goal context you provide or the app uses for general wellness, such as calorie awareness and macro tracking preferences.
  • App settings, including onboarding state, demo mode, language preferences, and local subscription entitlement state.
  • Subscription status and purchase information provided by Apple StoreKit.
  • Support messages you send to us, including your email address and the content of your request.

Photos and Camera

ClarioMeal may request access to the camera or photo library only when you choose to capture or select a meal photo. Meal photos are used to provide nutrition estimates. You should review AI estimates before relying on them.

AI Processing

AI meal analysis is handled through a secure server-side analysis service.

When you analyze a meal, the app may send the selected meal image and context such as locale and general goal context to ClarioMeal's analysis service. The service uses an AI provider to generate structured nutrition estimates and returns the result to the app.

AI estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not include sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential information in meal photos or prompts.

How We Use Information

  • Estimate calories, macros, ingredients, and calorie ranges.
  • Show confidence scores and hidden-calorie warnings for oils, sauces, dressings, fried preparation, and portion ambiguity.
  • Provide correction prompts and adaptive nutrition insights.
  • Verify Pro subscription access.
  • Respond to support requests and improve app reliability.

On-Device Storage

Meal analyses and app settings may be stored locally on your device. If iCloud backup or device backup is enabled on your device, app data may be included according to your Apple settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. ClarioMeal receives subscription entitlement information from Apple so the app can unlock Pro features. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data only with service providers needed to operate app features:

  • An AI provider may receive meal image and request context through ClarioMeal's secure analysis service to generate meal analysis results.
  • Apple StoreKit processes subscription purchases and provides subscription entitlement information.

These providers are used only to provide app functionality. We require service providers that process data for ClarioMeal to provide the same or equal protection for the information they process on our behalf.

Data Retention

Local meal data remains in the app until you delete it or remove the app. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to your request and maintain support records. Server logs, if generated, are kept only as needed for security, debugging, and service reliability.

Children

ClarioMeal is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Choose whether to select or capture meal photos.
  • Delete local app data by deleting the app from your device.
  • Avoid AI analysis if you do not want meal photos processed through ClarioMeal's secure analysis service and AI provider.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.
  • Contact us with privacy requests or questions.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by ClarioMeal. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International Processing

Service providers used by ClarioMeal may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@asilarslan.com.
