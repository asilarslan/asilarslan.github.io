Chalkline Pool Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Chalkline Pool ("the game") is an offline 8-ball and 9-ball pool game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android, iOS or Steam.

We do not collect personal data

We do not ask for, collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts and does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Your data stays on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, language, aim guide) and statistics (matches played and won, best run) are saved only in the game's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the game or its data removes them.

Advertising (Android and iOS)

The Android and iOS versions show ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between matches, and an optional video you can choose to watch to retake a shot against the computer. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information and how you interact with the ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track you across other apps, so ads are not personalised with cross-app tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The Steam (desktop) version has no ads.

Internet connection

The game itself runs offline; the internet is used only to load ads. It does not connect to any server of ours. If you download the game from a store (Google Play, the App Store or Steam), that store's own privacy policy covers the download and any data the store itself handles.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
