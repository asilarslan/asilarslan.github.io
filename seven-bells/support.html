Support / Destek

Having trouble with Seven Bells to Harbor? Write to meltembkr@gmail.com and tell us your device, operating system and what happened. We usually answer within two working days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language? Tap EN / TR at the top of any screen.

My hint is recharging. Wait for it to fill, or on mobile watch an optional short ad for an instant hint.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Türkçe
