Privacy Policy

Seven Bells to Harbor · Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Seven Bells to Harbor ("the game", "we") handles on iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop (Steam).

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or location. Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising (iOS and Android only)

The mobile versions show ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad you can watch for an extra hint, and occasional full-screen ads between chapters. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS), IP address, general location, device and app information and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are personalised you are shown Google's consent form, and you can choose not to consent.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

The desktop (Steam) version shows no ads and makes no network connections other than those made by Steam itself.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all saved progress from your device. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete; for ad data, use your device's ad settings or contact Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
