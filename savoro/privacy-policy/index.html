Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Savoro is a guided cooking-learning app. This policy explains the limited information the app processes and the choices available to you.

Information Stored on Your Device

Savoro stores onboarding status, selected language, appearance preference, lesson progress, saved recipe identifiers, completed recipe identifiers, streak and reward progress locally on your device. This data is used to personalize the app and preserve your progress. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings.

Recipe and Language Service

The app requests recipes and language-specific content from the Savoro API hosted at asilarslan.com. Requests may include the selected language, recipe identifier, category, pagination values, and standard network information such as IP address, request time, browser or device network headers, and server logs generated by the hosting provider.

Savoro does not require an end-user account and does not send your saved recipes, cooking completion history, ingredient checklist state, profile name, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifier to the recipe API.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive entitlement and product-status information needed to unlock Savoro Plus. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Analytics and Advertising

This version does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use app activity for cross-app tracking.

Support Messages

If you contact support, we process the email address, message, diagnostic details, and attachments you choose to provide in order to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or sensitive health information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by Apple for purchases, GitHub Pages for these public support pages, and our web-hosting provider for API delivery, security, and operational logging.

Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains until you clear it through available app controls or delete the app. Operational server logs may be retained for a limited period for security, debugging, and reliability. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain support records.

Children

Savoro is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security and International Processing

We use reasonable measures to protect the service, but no internet or storage system is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards.

Your Choices

  • Change language and appearance in Profile.
  • Add or remove saved recipes.
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored app data.
  • Manage subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Contact us with a privacy or deletion request.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear here. For questions, email support@asilarslan.com.
