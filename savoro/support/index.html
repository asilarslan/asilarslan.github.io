Support

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Need help learning or cooking with Savoro? Start with the answers below or contact us.

Contact support

Email support@asilarslan.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Savoro version/build, selected app language, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful.

Common Questions

Why does a saved recipe not appear?

Tap the bookmark on the recipe detail screen, then open Saved. Saved recipe identifiers are stored locally. If the server removes a recipe, it may no longer appear even though its identifier remains saved.

How does Start Cooking work?

Open a recipe and tap Start Cooking. Use the ingredient checklist, follow each guided step, and use the kitchen timer when helpful. Keep the app open for timer updates and always supervise active heat directly.

How do I change language or dark mode?

Open Profile, then choose Language or Appearance. English is the default and fallback when a translation is unavailable.

How do I restore Savoro Plus?

Open the Savoro Plus screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase. It may take a moment for Apple to refresh entitlement status.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, select Savoro, and follow Apple's cancellation controls.

Why is content unavailable?

Check your internet connection and try again. Savoro includes fallback content, but current recipes and translations are delivered by the Savoro API. Temporary maintenance or network filtering may affect updates.

How should I report incorrect recipe information?

Email the recipe name, selected language, incorrect ingredient or step, and your suggested correction. For urgent food-safety concerns, stop using the affected instructions until reviewed.
