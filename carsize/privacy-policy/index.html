Car Size

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Car Size iOS app processes information when you search and compare vehicles, save favorites, use subscriptions, or contact support.

Information Stored on Your Device

Favorites, comparison selections, appearance settings, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Car Size does not require a Car Size account. Local app data remains on the device until you change it or delete the app, subject to your device backup settings.

Catalog Searches and Service Logs

Search terms and selected vehicle identifiers are sent to the Car Size catalog service so it can return matching vehicle information and comparisons. The service may process and retain limited request information such as the request path, search term, IP address, date and time, user agent, and error or security events.

This information is used to provide app functionality, prevent abuse, investigate failures, and maintain reliability. It is not used for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Subscriptions and Payments

Car Size Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple provides the app with subscription entitlement status. Car Size does not receive or store your full payment card details. Subscription management is available in Apple ID Settings.

Support Messages

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to answer your request, prevent abuse, and maintain support records.

Data Sharing

Car Size does not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers that are necessary to operate the catalog and support services, and by Apple for StoreKit purchases.

Data Retention

Server logs are kept only as reasonably necessary for operation, security, abuse prevention, and debugging. Support messages may be retained while a request is active and as reasonably needed for support records.

Children

Car Size is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Remove favorites and comparison selections in the app.
  • Delete local app data by deleting the app.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple ID Settings.
  • Contact support with a privacy or data request.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Car Size evolves. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.
