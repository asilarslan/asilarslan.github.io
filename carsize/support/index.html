Support

Last updated: August 20, 2026

For app issues, subscription questions, feedback, or feature requests, email support@asilarslan.com.

When contacting support: include your device model, iOS version, Car Size version, and a short description. Screenshots are helpful when they do not contain private information.

Common Questions

How do I compare vehicles?

Use Search or Favorites and tap the comparison control on at least two vehicles. Open the Compare tab, then switch between the available views and measurements.

Where are my favorites stored?

Favorites are stored locally on your device. They do not require a Car Size account.

How do I restore Car Size Pro?

Open Settings in Car Size and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, choose Subscriptions, and select Car Size. Apple manages billing and cancellation.

Why can a measurement differ?

Dimensions can vary by market, model year, trim, wheels, tires, and equipment. Confirm critical fit or purchasing measurements with the manufacturer.

What should I avoid sending?

Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information in support messages.
