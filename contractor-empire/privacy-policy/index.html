Contractor Empire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Contractor Empire has no accounts and we do not run any servers that collect your data. The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google processes some device data to deliver them. The iOS and desktop versions contain no ads and make no network requests.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves your progress (company name, money, reputation, contracts, finished buildings, upgrades, statistics, achievements) and your settings (volume, language, graphics quality, controls) only on your device, in the app's private storage. The game itself never sends this data anywhere. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Android version)

The Android version shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad on the project results screen (you choose to watch it for an in-game bonus) and occasional full-screen ads between projects. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which indicates approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions and diagnostics. Depending on your choices and region, Google and its partners may use this data to show personalised ads. This data goes directly to Google; we do not receive or store it.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your Ad Choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ads are requested, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in the system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Accounts, Analytics and Tracking by Us

The game does not require an account and contains no analytics SDKs of our own. Apart from the advertising described above, the game makes no network requests.

Permissions

On Android the game requests vibration (haptic feedback when you place pieces; you can turn it off in Settings) and, for ads, internet access, network state and the advertising ID permission.

Purchases

Contractor Empire contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. In-game money is fictional. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple, Google, or Steam; we do not receive your payment details.

Platform Services

If a version adds optional platform features such as Steam achievements, Game Center, Google Play Games, or cloud saves, those features are provided by the platform under its own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that release.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.

Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

Android sürümü Google AdMob üzerinden reklam gösterir: proje sonuç ekranında isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklam ve projeler arasında ara sıra tam ekran reklam. Reklamları sunmak, ölçmek ve sahteciliği önlemek için Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı reklam kimliği, IP adresi (yaklaşık konum), cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri ile reklam etkileşimleri gibi verileri işler; bu veriler doğrudan Google'a gider, biz almayız ve saklamayız. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de reklam istenmeden önce onayın sorulur ve Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik tercihleri'nden değiştirilebilir. Reklam kimliğini Android ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsin. iOS ve masaüstü sürümlerinde reklam ve internet bağlantısı yoktur. Uygulama içi satın alma yoktur. Oyun 13 yaş altı çocuklara yönelik değildir. Ayrıntı: Google Gizlilik Politikası. Sorular için: support@asilarslan.com.
