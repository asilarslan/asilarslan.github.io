Contractor Empire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Contractor Empire is an offline game. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves your progress (company name, money, reputation, contracts, finished buildings, upgrades, statistics, achievements) and your settings (volume, language, graphics quality, controls) only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device by the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No Network, Accounts, Ads or Analytics

The game does not make network requests, does not require an account, and contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not use an advertising identifier and do not track you across apps or websites.

Permissions

On Android the only permission requested is vibration, used for haptic feedback when you place pieces. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Purchases

Contractor Empire contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. In-game money is fictional. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple, Google, or Steam; we do not receive your payment details.

Platform Services

If a version adds optional platform features such as Steam achievements, Game Center, Google Play Games, or cloud saves, those features are provided by the platform under its own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that release.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.
