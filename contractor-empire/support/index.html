Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I use the crane?

On a phone or tablet use the on-screen stick and buttons; drag to orbit the camera and pinch to zoom. On a keyboard: A/D slew, W/S trolley, R/F hoist, Q/E rotate the load, Space attach or release, V precision view. Bring the piece over its glowing blueprint ghost; when the ghost turns green, lower it gently and release.

Why won't my piece lock in place?

The piece must be centred over its target, low enough and turned the right way. The on-screen hint tells you whether to lower more or rotate. Pieces that are still swinging score lower, so wait for the sway to settle.

Can someone else do the lifting?

Yes. Hire the crew operator (G or the crew button) to place the next piece automatically for a small fee. The crew can also help with digging, pouring and painting.

I have no contracts I can take

Some contracts unlock with reputation and completed projects. If everything is locked, a new family-home contract always appears on a free plot, so you can never get stuck.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only, saved automatically after every piece. Deleting the app removes your progress.
