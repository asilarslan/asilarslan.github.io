Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Harborworks is free and supported by ads. The game itself does not collect personal information; the mobile versions (Android and iOS) show ads from Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves your career (funds, reputation, progress, statistics, equipment upgrades) and your settings (volume, language, graphics quality, difficulty, accessibility options) only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device by the app. It may be included in device backups according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

On Android and iOS the game shows two kinds of ads: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch after a ship visit to double that visit's pay, and occasional full-screen ads between ship visits. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID (Android), IP address, device model and OS, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from the IP address. This data goes directly to Google; we do not receive it. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS the game does not ask to track you, so ads there are not personalised with the Apple advertising identifier. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. The desktop versions contain no ads.

No Accounts or Analytics

The game does not require an account and contains no analytics SDKs of its own.

Device Permissions

On Android the game may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Purchases

Harborworks contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or gambling mechanics. If the game is sold as a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Google, Apple, or Steam; we do not receive your payment details.

Platform Services

If a future version adds optional platform features such as Steam achievements, Game Center, Google Play Games, or cloud saves, those features are provided by the platform under its own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that release.

Support Messages

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Children

Harborworks is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for general and parental-guidance audiences.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if features change. The latest version will always appear here. Questions: support@asilarslan.com.
