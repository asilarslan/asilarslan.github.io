Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I play?

Move the crane with the left stick (or WASD / a controller), hold ▲ / ▼ (E / Q) to hoist. Lower the spreader onto a container with a floating marker, wait for the lights to turn green and tap LOCK (Space). Lift it, carry it to a slot of the marker's colour, lower gently and tap RELEASE.

Why won't it lock or release?

The game always tells you why: the spreader may be misaligned (the offset is shown in metres), not resting on the roof, or the slot breaks a cargo rule (reefers need powered slots, heavy boxes go on the ground tier, nothing on fragile cargo). Use FINE (Shift) for the last half metre.

The load keeps swinging

Ease off early and follow the load with the trolley. The Anti-sway upgrade and the Beginner licence make it calmer; Settings also has a reduced-sway option.

The ship won't let me lift a box

The ship would list too far. Work the other side of the ship or wait a moment for the ballast pumps.

Repair jobs

Lift the worn (rusty, smoking) machinery onto the orange repair dock, then fit each new unit onto the mount with the same code. Fitting needs more precision than a container.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only, automatically after every ship. Deleting the app removes your progress.
