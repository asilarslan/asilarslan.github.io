Help center

Support

Email support@asilarslan.com for help. Include your platform, OS version, Pulseboard version, and a concise description. Do not send passwords or payment information.

Search and Filters

Search matches signal titles. Category chips narrow the visible list. Tap All to remove the category filter and clear the search field to restore all results.

Watchlist

Tap the bookmark on a signal to save or remove it. The bookmark in the header switches to saved-only mode. Your watchlist remains on the device between launches. Open Settings and choose Clear watchlist to remove all saved items.

No Results

Confirm that saved-only mode is off, select All, and clear the search text. If the issue remains, close and reopen the app.

Privacy

Pulseboard does not require an account or collect personal information. Read the complete Privacy Policy.

Response target: Support requests are normally reviewed within five business days.
