Pulseboard

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 10, 2026

Pulseboard is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information.

Information We Collect

The Pulseboard mobile application does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not contain advertising, analytics, attribution, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Information Stored on Your Device

When you save a signal, its non-personal identifier is stored locally in Apple UserDefaults on iOS or SharedPreferences on Android. This information is used only to display your watchlist. You can clear it in Pulseboard Settings or by deleting the app. Pulseboard does not provide an account or cloud synchronization.

Network Access and Permissions

The current app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo-library, Bluetooth, health, or notification permission. Core trend content is bundled with the app and the app does not require network access for its main functionality. Opening Privacy Policy, Terms, or Support launches your system browser at your request.

Website Hosting

This policy and our support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit them in a browser, GitHub may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website processing is separate from the Pulseboard mobile app.

Children's Privacy

Pulseboard is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and Retention

Because the app does not receive your watchlist, we cannot access it. Locally stored preferences remain until you clear them or remove the app, subject to your device backup settings.

Your Choices and Rights

You can use all primary features without providing personal information. You may clear the local watchlist at any time. For privacy questions or rights requests concerning a support email you sent, contact us using the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy if Pulseboard's features or data practices change. The current version and date will always be published at this URL before materially different data processing is introduced.

Contact
