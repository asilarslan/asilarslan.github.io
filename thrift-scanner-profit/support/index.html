Help center

Support

For help with Thrift Scanner, email support@asilarslan.com. Include your Android version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Never email payment-card details.

Get Better Resale Research

  • Photograph the complete item in bright, even light.
  • Include labels, maker marks, serial numbers, accessories, and visible flaws.
  • Add dimensions, working condition, and missing pieces in a follow-up message.
  • Verify current sold listings instead of relying on asking prices.

Camera or Gallery

If the camera is unavailable, open Android Settings, find Thrift Scanner, select Permissions, and allow Camera access. You can also select an existing image through Gallery.

Analysis or Network Errors

Confirm that the device has an internet connection, choose a clear image, and retry. If the issue continues, include the approximate time of the error in your support email.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Confirm that you are signed into the Google Play account used for purchase. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing devices.

Manage, Cancel, or Request a Refund

Open Google Play, tap your profile, choose Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Select Thrift Scanner to manage or cancel. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Refund eligibility is determined under Google Play rules.

Privacy Requests

For privacy or deletion requests, email support@asilarslan.com. Review the Privacy Policy for details.
