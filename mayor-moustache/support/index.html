Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I play?

Drag with one finger to move around the town, pinch to zoom, and use the Rotate buttons to turn the view. Pick a building card from the build bar, then tap a green lot to place it. Tap a building to collect its rent or upgrade it. On a computer: WASD to pan, the mouse wheel to zoom, Q/E to rotate.

How do I earn more money?

Homes pay rent; tap the coins above them or use Collect all. Happy residents pay more, so put parks, cafés and the bandstand near homes, keep workshops a little further away, and add a clinic and a school once homes reach level 2. Each morning brings taxes minus service upkeep.

What is the happiness map?

It colours every lot by how pleasant it is to live there, so you can see where new homes will be happiest. While placing a building, its area of effect is shown too.

Where is my town saved?

On your device only. The game saves automatically every morning, when you leave the app and from the pause menu. Deleting the app removes your town.

The Android back button

Back closes the open panel, cancels building, or opens the pause menu. On the title screen it closes the game.

Language

The game is in English and Turkish and follows your device language on first launch. Change it any time in Settings → Language.
