Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Ring Sizer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There is no account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your saved sizes, names you give them and your settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • The app works offline. It only talks to the App Store when you subscribe to Ring Sizer Pro, restore a purchase or check your subscription.
  • Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, email address or payment details.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact: support@asilarslan.com
