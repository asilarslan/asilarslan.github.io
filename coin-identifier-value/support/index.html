Help center

Support

For help with Coin Identifier & Value, email support@asilarslan.com. Include your Android version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Never email payment-card details.

Getting a Better Identification

  • Photograph the coin straight-on in bright, even light.
  • Keep the full rim visible and avoid glare or heavy shadows.
  • Submit clear photos of both obverse and reverse when possible.
  • Include diameter, weight, edge details, and visible inscriptions in a follow-up message.
  • Do not clean a potentially valuable coin to improve the photo.

Camera Access

If the camera is unavailable, open Android Settings, find Coin Identifier & Value, select Permissions, and allow Camera access. You can also use the gallery option to select an existing image.

Identification or Network Errors

Confirm that your device has an internet connection, choose a clear image, and retry. If the issue continues, include the approximate time of the error in your support email.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Confirm you are signed into the Google Play account used for the purchase. Use Restore Purchases inside the app after reinstalling or changing devices.

Manage or Cancel

Open Google Play, tap your profile, choose Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Select Coin Identifier & Value to manage or cancel. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Refund eligibility and billing disputes are handled according to Google Play rules. Use your Google Play order history or Google Play support for a refund request.

Privacy and Deletion Requests

For a privacy or server-image deletion request, email support@asilarslan.com with the approximate submission date and any details that can help locate the request. Review the Privacy Policy for more information.

Reminder: App results are research estimates, not professional authentication, certified grading, or appraisal.
