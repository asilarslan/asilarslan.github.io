Coin Identifier & Value

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This policy explains how Coin Identifier & Value processes information when you capture or select a coin image, request AI-assisted research, use subscriptions, or contact support.

Information You Provide

When you request an identification, the app processes the image you capture or select, your research request, and follow-up messages. Photos can contain the coin, its holder, surroundings, or other visible details. Avoid including faces, addresses, documents, or unnecessary personal information.

Camera and Photo Access

The app requests camera access so you can photograph a coin and uses Android's system photo selection experience when you choose an existing image. Access is used after your action to provide these features. Camera permission can be changed in Android Settings.

AI Analysis and Service Providers

Your selected image and related messages are transmitted over HTTPS to our server and then to OpenAI for visual analysis and response generation. OpenAI processes this content as a service provider under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Do not submit content you are not authorized to share.

Server Image Storage

Uploaded images are temporarily made technically accessible from our server using non-descriptive, hash-based filenames so the analysis service can retrieve them. Directory listing is not intended to be public, but anyone who obtains the exact image URL may be able to access it.

Images may remain until removed for operational, storage, security, or privacy reasons or following a valid deletion request. Contact us with the approximate submission date and any available details if you want to request deletion.

Information Stored on Your Device

Onboarding status, usage state, subscription state, scan history, image references, and conversations may be stored locally. The app does not require a Coin Identifier & Value account. Local information remains until removed through the app, Android settings, device backup behavior, or app deletion.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. The app receives product information and purchase or entitlement state needed to provide Premium access. We do not receive or store complete payment-card details.

Technical Data and Logs

Hosting providers may process limited technical information such as IP address, request time, user agent, requested path, response status, and security or error events to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Support Communications

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message, and anything else you choose to provide. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, prevent abuse, and maintain support records.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed with providers supporting app operation, including hosting providers, OpenAI for analysis, and Google Play for billing and distribution, or where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Retention and Deletion

Retention depends on information type and operational need. Server logs and support records are kept only as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, legal compliance, and support. You may request access or deletion by contacting us. Additional details may be required to locate a submission.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal information.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS and restricted access to server credentials. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, information is handled using appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Choose whether to grant camera permission or select an image.
  • Avoid submitting unnecessary personal information.
  • Delete local app data through Android settings or app deletion.
  • Manage subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Contact us to exercise applicable privacy rights or request deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app or its service providers change. The current version and update date will be published here.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support@asilarslan.com.
