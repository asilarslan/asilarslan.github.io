Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Tan Maxxing is designed to help users plan tanning sessions, track UV-aware exposure, log progress, and receive educational AI coaching. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may process, how it is used, and what choices users have.

Information We Process

  • Profile choices you select in onboarding or settings, such as skin type, tanning goal, sensitivity, sunscreen level, preferred sun window, and reminder preferences.
  • UV index values you enter manually or request through an optional server UV endpoint.
  • Session logs you save, such as date, duration, UV index, SPF, front/back timing, recovery hours, and notes.
  • Active timer state, such as the current session plan and start time, so the app can resume an in-progress session if it is closed and reopened.
  • AI coach requests you type, together with selected profile choices, UV value, and recent session summaries if you enable AI processing.
  • Subscription status and purchase information provided by Apple StoreKit.
  • Support messages you send to us, including your email address and message content.

Photos, Camera, and Identity Data

Tan Maxxing does not request camera access or photo library access in this version.

The app does not upload selfies, face images, body photos, contacts, names, or device advertising identifiers to the AI proxy. Skin profile setup is based on self-selected answers on your device.

AI Processing

Tan Maxxing does not store an OpenAI API key in the iOS app.

If you enable AI processing and use an AI feature, the app may send your selected profile choices, UV value, recent session summaries, and typed question to Tan Maxxing's server-side PHP proxy. The proxy forwards the text request to OpenAI to generate an educational plan or coach reply.

Do not include sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential information in AI coach messages.

How We Use Information

  • Create tanning plans and timers.
  • Track session progress.
  • Resume active timers when the app is reopened.
  • Generate educational AI coach replies.
  • Verify subscription access through Apple StoreKit.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain reliability and security.

On-Device Storage

Profile choices, active timer state, and session logs are stored on your device using local app storage. If iCloud or device backup is enabled, app data may be included according to your Apple settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data only with service providers needed to operate app features:

  • OpenAI receives AI request text through Tan Maxxing's server-side PHP proxy only after you enable AI processing.
  • Apple StoreKit processes subscriptions and entitlement status.
  • Open-Meteo may receive latitude and longitude if you use an optional UV endpoint in a version that enables location-based UV lookup.

Data Retention

Profile and session data remain on your device until you delete the app or clear the data. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond and maintain support records. Server logs, if generated, are kept only as needed for security, debugging, and reliability.

Health and Safety

Tan Maxxing provides educational planning only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Stop sun exposure if you notice redness, pain, itching, dizziness, unusual skin changes, or medication-related concerns, and consult a qualified professional.

Children

Tan Maxxing is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Edit profile settings in the app.
  • Delete session logs.
  • Turn AI processing consent on or off.
  • Avoid AI features if you do not want profile choices or typed questions processed by the server-side proxy and OpenAI.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.
  • Contact support with privacy questions.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Tan Maxxing. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact
