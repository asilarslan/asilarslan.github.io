Common Questions

Why does the app not ask for camera or photo library access?

This version does not use selfie, body photo, or skin photo analysis. Tan Maxxing uses profile choices, SPF, UV values, and session logs instead.

How do I resume a sun session?

If you start a session and close the app, open Tan Maxxing again and tap Resume sun session. The timer uses the original start time.

Why is my Lock Screen timer not visible?

Live Activities must be enabled on your iPhone. Open iOS Settings, check app permissions for Live Activities, and make sure the session timer is running.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings in Tan Maxxing, view Premium plans, and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select Tan Maxxing.

What should I avoid sending to support?

Do not send sensitive medical information, private photos, identity documents, payment details, or confidential information in support messages.
