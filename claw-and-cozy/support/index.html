Support

Need help or found a bug? Email support@asilarslan.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened.

How do I play?

Tap a plush (or use the joystick / WASD / a controller) to move the claw over it. The shadow ring shows exactly where the claw will land. Press Drop to grab. Press Drop again while descending to grab early.

Why did my plush slip?

Grabs depend on centering, the plush's size and weight, whether it is lying on its side, toys piled on top, and how much the claw swings. Aim for the middle of upright plushies, and upgrade Grip and Stability for heavy prizes.

I ran out of coins

Customers keep visiting your arcade and paying to play, and every new day gives you free plays. Tap Close for the day in your arcade to start a fresh day.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app removes your progress. You can reset progress in Settings → Game (a backup copy is kept).

Accessibility

Settings include reduced motion, screen-shake toggle, a stronger aim guide, text size, vibration, and rebindable keys.
