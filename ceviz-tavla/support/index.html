Ceviz Tavla – Support

Offline backgammon (tavla) on a walnut board for Android, iOS and Steam. Play the computer on three levels or a friend on the same device.

How to play

  • Roll: tap the board or the Roll button.
  • Move: tap a checker; golden spots show where it can go. If there is only one place, it moves straight away. You can also drag.
  • Undo / Done: take moves back until you tap Done.
  • Replay the animated guide any time from How to play in the menu.

Rules in short

Move all fifteen checkers into your home board, then bear them off. A single checker can be hit and sent to the bar; it must re-enter before anything else moves. Doubles are played four times. You must use as many dice as possible, and the larger die when only one can be played. A win where the opponent has borne off nothing is a gammon (mars) and counts two points.
